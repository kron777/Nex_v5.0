DEFINITION 1 -- "hard scenario": A consecutive turn pair (T_n, T_n+1)
satisfying all four conditions: (1) Commitment in T_n -- a named entity, a
chosen option among explicit alternatives, a number, or a stated stance; a
generic theme is not a commitment. (2) No restatement in T_n+1's own input
-- load-bearing: if restated, carry is unobservable. (3) Genuine turn
boundary -- a separate turn, not a continuation of the same user message.
(4) Carry-eligible by design -- exists in prior state and not excluded from
carry by design; if excluded by design, record as excluded with reason.

DEFINITION 2 -- carryover scoring rule: Binary CARRY=1 or CARRY=0. CARRY=1
iff T_n+1's output contains at least one element traceable to the T_n
commitment which cannot be derived from T_n+1's own input, AND the trace is
quoted in both directions. No partial credit, no inferred continuity.
Direction of error declared in advance: strict, under-counts rather than
over-counts; a single CARRY=1 is sufficient to confirm Fork A.
