treated as valid evidence of topical overlap is the single largest
identified driver of Round 5's inflated numbers. My original rule
counted ANY shared word of length >=4 as a match, with no exclusion
list. Recomputing the full 2994-3014-fire aggregate with stricter
variants of that same rule (exact queries and outputs below) moves both
arms down substantially, in the independent run's direction:
  Original rule (>=4 chars, no exclusions) -- Round 5's number:
    observed 21.009%, predicted 30.873%, gap -9.86pp.
  Hand-built ~45-word generic exclusion list, same >=4-char floor:
    observed 8.726%, predicted 15.607%, gap -6.88pp. (classifiable=3014)
  Pure length threshold, no hand list, minlen=5:
    observed 9.224%, predicted 16.981%, gap -7.76pp. (classifiable=3014)
  Pure length threshold, minlen=6:
    observed 6.587%, predicted 12.213%, gap -5.63pp. (classifiable=3006)
  Pure length threshold, minlen=7:
    observed 4.435%, predicted 7.944%, gap -3.51pp. (classifiable=2954)
None of these four variants exactly reproduces the independent run's
stated 13.4%/22.7% -- that number sits between my original (21.0/30.9)
and my minlen=5 variant (9.2/17.0), closer to my minlen=4-with-exclusion-
list result (8.7/15.6) and minlen=5 result but still visibly higher than
both. This means the independent run's actual rule is neither my
original rule nor any of these four straightforward corrections --
it very likely differs in some additional, specific way (e.g. a
