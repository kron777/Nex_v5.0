## 1. Project identity and evidence discipline

NEX is an experimental persistent cognitive architecture investigating how memory, attention, belief formation, self-observation, environmental experience and language-model participation can operate as an integrated system.

Do not equate:
- a design document with implemented code;
- source presence with runtime execution;
- a database write with downstream consumption;
- runtime activity with effective behavioural validation;
- convincing language with proof of subjective experience.

When describing a capability, state the evidence level and identify the source, test, runtime observation or controlled experiment supporting it. Preserve uncertainty and competing explanations.

## 2. Authority and development controls

This guide does not replace founder-approved governance. Before development, retrieve and follow the current approved master handover, roadmap, frozen specifications and founder decisions. If an authoritative document or approval record cannot be located, stop at that decision boundary rather than infer approval from code, PR descriptions or old documents.

The latest recorded development position in the 2026-11-23 handover is **Phase 0: Reality Audit and End-to-End Causal Trace**. Phase 1 behavioural work remains closed pending Gate 0 approval. RDO and Watcher are external development instruments, not NEX cognitive faculties.

Do not merge PRs, change live runtime state, alter protected experiments/baselines/tripwires, or change frozen specifications without the required approval.

## 5. Generated and dated documents

- `CORPUS.md` is generated by `scripts/nexsnap.py`; do not hand-edit it. Its embedded generation date must be checked before relying on its snapshot.
- `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `SENTIENCE_PROGRAM.md`, `README.md` and subsystem specifications contain dated status claims. Source and current runtime evidence take precedence over stale status prose, subject to the formal authority hierarchy.
- `journal/CARRY_OVER.md`, `SNAPSHOT_FINDINGS.md`, `TRACK_THEORY.md`, `SUBSTRATE_NOTES.md` and experiment reports preserve reasoning/evidence. Do not delete them simply because they are old or lengthy.
- `tests/known_failures.txt` and all `requirements*.txt` files are operational inputs, not disposable notes.

## 7. Archive policy

The `archive/` tree holds dated handovers, old prompts, superseded planning documents, prior audits and old positions that retain historical value. Archived material is evidence/history, not current instructions. See `archive/README.md`.

Delete a document only when it is genuinely redundant, generated noise, or safely superseded and no code, test, workflow, documentation link, experiment or decision depends on it. Prefer archiving when it contains unique reasoning or experimental history.

## 8. Working discipline

- Inspect the current branch, commit, working tree and actual source before acting.
- Check upstream preconditions, shared readers, existing mechanisms and proof of non-regression before changing a wire.
- Reuse existing capabilities before proposing new mechanisms.
- Right-size each change; avoid broad refactors and speculative nodes.
- Separate verified facts, inferences, hypotheses and unknowns.
- Keep experiments, baselines and falsifiers intact.
- Record exact files, commits, tests and evidence.
- Do not let this guide or any generated summary override founder-approved authority.
