# D. Kill shape — DESIGN ONLY, NOT SHIPPED — REASONED

## D1 — R32's dedup does not apply, and not for the reason the brief expects

The brief asks whether `advancements` escapes R32 by being an abstraction rather than a
near-duplicate. **It does not escape R32 — it never reaches it.**

R32's dedup (`generator.py:1748-1769`, first-sentence 5-gram Jaccard ≥ 0.5) guards the
**exemplar pool**, which is read **only in the DRIFT branch**. The groove is **98.8%
EXPLAIN/ARGUE**. It travels a path R32 does not cover, and the hot-observer slot that carries
it has **no dedup at all**.

**And the crystallizer's own semantic gate misses it on scope, not on threshold:**

```python
def _was_recently_semantically_similar(self, content, minutes=30, threshold=0.85)
```

| | |
|---|---|
| gate window | **30 minutes** |
| groove inter-arrival, median | **213 minutes** |
| **consecutive pairs inside the 30-min window** | **0 of 15** |
| consecutive pairs inside 24 h | **15 of 15** |

**Not one instance was ever catchable.** The gate is built for rapid-fire repetition; the
groove runs at a few per day. **This is the same defect class as R71/R72 — a gate whose
window is far shorter than the phenomenon it must catch** — and it is the third instance in
five rounds.

## D2 — smallest intervention, with its damper

**Widen the crystallizer's similarity window from 30 minutes to 24 hours**, keeping
`threshold=0.85` and raising the `LIMIT 20` so the window is actually covered (~72
fountain beliefs/day). One function's parameters.

**The damper, and it comes straight out of C1:** only reject when the two similar beliefs
have **different `focal_item`s**. That is precisely the contamination signature — the same
proposition welded to unrelated material — and it **exempts genuine sustained engagement**
with one subject, which is behaviour worth keeping. A groove is repetition *across*
subjects; depth is repetition *within* one.

**Second candidate, smaller and narrower:** apply a similarity check to the hot-observer
slot, or stop hot observations quoting fountain text verbatim. **This is closer to the root**
— B3 shows the slot is the amplifier — but it changes a live self-observation path and
deserves its own round.

**Neither is shipped, and I am not recommending one over the other yet.** The measurement
that would decide it: whether the groove persists once the similarity gate can see it. **The
threshold question is whether this warrants a fire-path change at all while the token sits
one point under threshold and has not breached.**

## D3 — tripwire, if the decision is to wait

Since nothing is being shipped, the honest alternative is a watch condition:

1. **`advancements` DF exceeds 25% on any full day**, or maxDF\* breaches on it → it has
   ignited; intervene.
2. **A third proposition appears** — the fused pair sheds "mirror societal changes" and picks
   up new content while the tokens persist → the mechanism is confirmed self-sustaining and
   the case for D2 is made.
3. **Both tokens fall under 10% for 3 consecutive days** → it decayed like societal after
   all; close it out and no fix is warranted.
