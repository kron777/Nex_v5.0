## B. The label stall — the decision for Jon

### B.1 A measurement that changes the question — OBSERVED

Before recommending anything I tested the assumption the whole debate rests on: **that the −1.577 is still doing live harm through the exemplar pool.**

R32 fixed *duplication* in the pool. It did not change what the pool *ranks* on — the top-10 are still chosen by the contaminated score. So the pool should still skew toward templated, low-novelty fires. **It does not:**

| instant (08-08 → 08-10, every 6 h) | pool mean F2 | population mean F2 | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 instants | 0.946 – 0.977 | 0.887 – 0.939 | **+0.012 to +0.069** |
| **mean** | | | **+0.0482** |

**The pool runs HIGHER novelty than the population on 12 of 12 instants.** The reason is structural: R32's dedup step drops candidates whose first sentence overlaps a kept one at 5-gram Jaccard ≥ 0.5 — **the dedup is itself a novelty filter, and it dominates the score-based ranking.**

**R32 did not just break the amplification loop; it structurally neutralised the negative weight's effect on the one path that reached fire content.** This is the opposite of what I expected to find, and it is label-free.

### B.2 The three paths, with honest risk

**(a) Flip on the mechanism, no labels, tight falsifier + instant revert.**
- *In favour:* revert is genuinely instant — `tagger._load_weights()` hot-reloads on mtime, so the save is the deploy and the undo is one file write. R48's mechanism is label-free and solid.
- *Against, and it is decisive:* **no defensible magnitude exists.** Candidate values span −0.7116 (multivariate refit on the same 103 rows — **still negative**), 0.0, +0.5245, +0.7270, +1.5514. **The one honest refit of the original training data reproduces a negative sign.** R48 shows the negative weight *has a bad consequence*; it does not show a positive weight is *correct*.
- The blast radius is large: **neutralising F2 alone moves STRIKING +25.6 pp (14.0% → 39.6%); the positive candidates move it +30.5 to +54.0 pp.** That is a regime change in the primary output, not a tweak.
- **And with no labels there is no endpoint that measures the thing you care about.** You would pre-register a proxy (contaminant enrichment, maxDF\*) and then optimise the proxy. **§B.1 has just removed the best proxy by showing the pool is already fine.**

**(b) Wait for labels.**
- *Cost of waiting is much lower than it was.* The standing argument for urgency was that a bad scorer keeps feeding the exemplar pool. **§B.1 shows it no longer does.** R32's dedup is a structural guard that holds regardless of whether the weight is right.
- *Risk:* indefinite. Labels have failed twice — the empty-input run, then κ=0 on my own labels (R43). R42's fresh blind set is drawn and unscored.
- *What breaks:* the scorer stays a poor genius-detector for its stated purpose. That matters only to consumers of the STRIKING label, and the main one is now defended.

**(c) Leave the sign permanently and move on.**
- *Risk:* a deployed weight that contradicts its own feature docstring stays in the codebase, and anyone reading it later re-opens this.
- *What breaks:* less than it looks like. With the pool guarded, the sign's practical reach is small.
- *Cost:* you would be closing a known-wrong-looking thing on the grounds that it is harmless, which requires §B.1 to keep holding — it depends on the dedup staying in place. **If the dedup is ever removed or bypassed, the harm channel reopens immediately.**

### B.3 Recommendation — REASONED, and it is not a ship

**Recommend (b), leaning (c) — and explicitly NOT (a).** The case for flipping rested on urgency that §B.1 has measured away, and the flip has no defensible magnitude. **The sign is not worth the label cost on its own any more.** Labels are still worth collecting for the broader question of whether the scorer tracks anything real, but that is a different and larger question than one weight's sign.

**If the sign is ever revisited, do the reference-freeze first, not the flip.** R39 measured **99.97%** of F2's variance as its own moving window; R49 measured fixed-reference sd **0.00154** against rolling **0.08801**. A feature that measures its own reference frame cannot be fitted stably — which is exactly why the same data yielded −1.577 and −0.430. **Freezing the reference is a change to the feature's definition, not its weight, needs no labels, and would make any future fit reproducible.** It does change scorer output and therefore fire content, so it is Jon's call — but it is a better first move than the flip.

**Nothing was flipped. No weight file was touched.**
