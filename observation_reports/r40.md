**Where they come apart, concretely:**

1. **Base rate.** I marked 3.3% striking; the deployed scorer marks ~15%; Jon's training set was ~65% striking by construction (67 of 103). If Jon's threshold for "striking" is looser than mine, every accuracy number here shifts and the *sign* of the disagreement could invert.

3. **Accuracy on an imbalanced set is dominated by the negative class.** With 116 negatives, a labeller who marks almost everything ordinary trivially scores 100% on low anchors and 0% on high anchors — which is exactly the shape below. **§B.2's headline number is therefore partly a fact about my strictness.**

## D. What I would do — my call

### D.1 Ranked

1. **Fix the scorer's F2 sign — after C2's read, and only with more labels.** Highest leverage in the system: it selects the exemplar pool, and B.2 shows it currently selects the contaminant at 2.5× enrichment. Needs ~300 labels to fit, or a defensible a-priori argument for the sign (which the feature's own docstring arguably supplies: *"1.0 = novel"* with a negative weight is on its face a bug, not a fit).
2. **Retire the F2 rolling window, or freeze its reference.** R39 measured **99.97%** of F2's variance as the moving window rather than the fires. A feature that measures its own reference frame cannot be fitted stably — this is why `ab1b8a6` saw −1.577 vs −0.430 on the same data. Fix this *before* any refit or the refit will not hold either.
3. **The C2 read**, 2026-08-10T16:36:43Z. Already pre-registered.
4. **The quiescent raw-dump path** (`generator.py:800`): 19.8% of all fires are verbatim feed payloads. Not a defect — a design whose cost nobody had priced. Decide whether 1-in-5 is the right rate.
5. **maxDF\*'s register list**, still fitted across the R29 regime change.

### D.2 Retire

- **mode=None as its own round — YES, retire it.** R39 §A.6 explains it: 31.4% of those fires are raw feed dumps, which by construction contain no first person and fail the engagement gate. The remaining failure is ordinary output that happens not to use "I". There is no mystery left, only the design decision in D.1.4. **Fold it into that and close the item.**
- **The coingecko fix** — already dropped in R39; the feed was never broken.
- **The groove-filter concentration replay.** R32 removed the filter's power to concentrate anything by deduping the pool ahead of sampling. The hypothesis is now unfalsifiable *and* moot. **Retire it.**

### D.3 What I would do differently — blunt

**I would stop shipping one change per round.** Rounds 32–39 shipped nine changes and produced two clean attributions (R28, R32). The binding constraint was never engineering time — it was that most rounds had no endpoint that could move, so the discipline was theatre in those rounds and rigour in the two that mattered. I would run *measurement-only* rounds explicitly labelled as such, and reserve the full apparatus for changes with a live endpoint.

**I would have built the instruments first.** Every genuine finding in rounds 36–40 came from an instrument built late: the prompt manifest (R37), the error sink (R33, which found the coingecko noise), `focal_item` logging (R23, which is why fidelity is unmeasurable before 2026-08-02), the feature baseline (R39). The four errors I have had to correct in my own rounds were all *inference from missing data*, not bad reasoning over good data. Three days of instrumentation up front would have been worth more than the first twenty rounds of intervention.

**I would have checked the scorer at round 1.** It has been the upstream cause the whole time. R31 and R32 spent two rounds on the exemplar pool; B.2 shows the pool was full of contaminant because the scorer *preferred* it. We treated the symptom for two rounds because nobody asked whether the selector worked.

**And the honest one: I have been too willing to accept a brief's framing.** R38's coingecko ranking, R38's C4 "a refit would mask it", R36's "unattributed halving" — each time I built on a premise handed to me instead of testing it first. The rounds where I checked the premise (R32's predicate, R37's DB, R39's R28 reconstruction) are the ones that produced results. **The brief is a hypothesis, not a specification, and I should treat it that way by default.**
