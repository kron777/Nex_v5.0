**But it will if the check is run now**, by whoever has read that message. The leak is un-fired, not harmless.

### A.3 By how much — this part I can quantify exactly

With my labels at **2 striking / 18 ordinary**, raw percent-agreement over 20 items is bounded as follows — OBSERVED, this is arithmetic, not estimation:

| if the human marks k striking | possible agreement | clears R40 §E's ≥80%? |
|---|---|---|
| k=0 | 90% | **always** |
| k=1 | 85–95% | **always** |
| **k=2** | **80–100%** | **always** |
| k=3 | 75–95% | sometimes |
| k=4 | 70–90% | sometimes |

**Any labeller who marks 0, 1 or 2 of the 20 striking clears the "R40 carries over" threshold mechanically — regardless of whether they agree with me about *which* fires are striking.** I told him the answer was 2.

So the harm is precise: **the leak makes R40 §E's pre-registered ≥80% band effectively unfalsifiable.** A run performed now cannot distinguish "Jon agrees with me" from "Jon landed on the disclosed ratio".

**What I cannot tell:** whether Jon would have anchored. He may have ignored it entirely, or never read it. Anchoring is a tendency, not a certainty, and with n=1 human there is no way to detect it after the fact. **That unknowability is the damage** — not that anchoring definitely occurred, but that the run can no longer rule it out.

### A.4 A second defect the leak exposed — my metric was already weak

**REASONED, and it is my error from R40, independent of the leak:** raw percent-agreement is the wrong statistic for a 2/18 split. It is dominated by the negative class. Two labellers who each mark 2 striking but share **none** of them still score 80% and "pass".

| both mark 2 striking, overlap | raw agreement | Cohen's κ |
|---|---|---|
| 2 of 2 | 100% | **1.00** |
| 1 of 2 | 90% | **0.44** |
| 0 of 2 | 80% | **−0.11** |

κ separates these cleanly where raw agreement does not. **R40 §E's three bands were badly constructed and I would not have caught it without making the leak.**

### A.5 The rule, written down

Added to `theory_x/genius/JUDGING_RULE_r40.md`:

> **Before a human runs a blind check, they get NO aggregate statistics, NO label counts or ratios, NO indication of which items are contested or disagreed-on, and NO guidance on what to watch for. None. Not "minimal hints" — none. The only permitted disclosure is the mechanics: how to run it, what the keys do, and how the result will be read.**

Plus the metric fix: blind checks report **Cohen's κ and positive-class agreement**; raw agreement may appear alongside, never alone.
