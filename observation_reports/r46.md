## A. R45's lesson applied backward — no landmine, but three load-bearing precedents

**Verdict: no R45-style landmine. Continuing to B/C, with constraints.**

The R45 failure mode was specific: *proposing to reconnect something that had been deliberately disconnected.* I searched for that shape on traversal and did not find it.

**`belief_edges` / `last_traversed_at` — OBSERVED.** Only one substantive commit: `f92589a` (2026-04-23) *"Belief edge graph + spreading activation"*, which built the table, `ActivationEngine`, and a `BeliefRetriever` doing a *"60/40 activation/keyword blend when edges exist"*. **No commit ever disabled or reverted it.** `BeliefRetriever` is called from exactly one place — `stage_capability/tools.py:169`, the chat/tool path. So spreading activation was **built for the query path and never extended to the autonomous one**. Never-connected, not deliberately-unwired. That is the distinction R45 turned on, and it comes out the other way here.

**But three prior decisions constrain the design, and two are direct structural precedents:**

**A.1 — `ea6fe74` (2026-05-12) "remove recent-thoughts echo loop (Mechanism A)".** The fountain was injecting recent `fountain_events` as exemplars; *"every fire was being few-shot-prompted with 5 copies of the lock"*. Removed entirely. Cites a **standing rule: "no call reordering in `_build_prompt()`"**. Design 2 adds retrieval candidates rather than reordering prompt blocks, so it does not violate that rule — but the lesson (feeding her own material back concentrates motifs) applies directly.

**A.2 — `6182033` (2026-05-19) "residue-ghost groove root cause". This is the closest structural analogue to Design 2 and it is a warning.** Residue is a **prepend** mechanism into `_retrieve_context_beliefs`, popped via `ORDER BY activation_strength DESC LIMIT 2`. **70,439 ghost rows** accumulated holding *"long-dead boost values from weeks ago"*, because *"residue entries are never updated when the underlying belief's boost changes"*. Those stale snapshots dominated the prepend queue and produced the *"attending to quiet"* deep groove.

> **Binding constraint on Design 2: a prepend queue ranked by a stored score that is never refreshed produces a groove.** Edge `weight` is exactly such a score — written once at edge creation, never updated. **Design 2 must not rank candidates by `weight`.**

**A.3 — `9cbf4e5` "HOT retrieval: dedicated slot bypassing recency race".** Adding `hot_observer` to `_OWN_CONTENT_SOURCES` failed because *"newer beliefs from high-cadence sources (fires dozens/hr) drowned HOT (fires ~4/hr) out of the oversample pool"*. The fix was a **dedicated slot**, not a shared pool.

> **Binding constraint: traversed neighbours must not be merged into the shared oversample.** They would either drown the existing sources or be drowned by them. They need their own slot.

## B. The refractory-window claim — stress-tested and partly FALSIFIED

R45 closed by claiming `last_traversed_at` is *"rate-adequate by construction"*. **That claim does not survive contact with the edge counts.**

### B.2 Demand vs supply, at 529 fires/day — OBSERVED

| fan-out | demand/day | semantic pool (893) | all edges (135,798) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 529 | **1.69 d full cycle** | 256.7 d |
| 2 | 1,058 | **0.84 d** | 128.4 d |
| 3 | 1,587 | **0.56 d** | 85.6 d |
| 5 | 2,645 | **0.34 d** | 51.3 d |

**Answer to B.1's question: yes, and the rate is low.** Restricted to the 893 semantically-meaningful edges, **the entire eligible set is consumed in under two days at any fan-out.** A refractory window longer than ~1 day exhausts the pool (traversal returns nothing); shorter, and the same 893 edges recycle continuously — **a slower-motion version of exactly the loop the window was meant to prevent.** R45's "by construction" claim was wrong: a refractory window bounds *per-edge* frequency but does nothing about *set* concentration when the set is small.

**The full 135,798-edge pool has ample headroom (51–257 days).** But R33 established 99.3% of it is `cross_domain`/`synthesises` written on raw token-overlap ≥2/≥3 — near-noise.

### B.3 And the filters are mutually exclusive — OBSERVED

| weight ≥ | all edges | semantic edges |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 127,322 | 356 |
| 0.6 | 126,243 | 80 |
| **0.7** | 126,122 | **0** |
| 0.9 | 25,434 | 0 |

**Every semantic edge has weight < 0.7.** A quality filter on `weight` and a filter on `edge_type` cannot be combined — above 0.7 there are no `supports`/`refines`/`opposes` edges at all. Any design must pick one axis.

### B.4 A third blocker: there is no anchor — OBSERVED

Design 2 says "traverse outward from the current focal item". **There is nothing to traverse from.**

- `focal_item` is a **sense-item string**, not a belief id.
- `anchor_belief_id` is **NULL on all 7,141 fires in the last 14 days**. It was written 2026-05-21 → **2026-07-13 (27 days ago), n=1,187 total**, and only by the `substrate_voice` path (`generator.py:665`). The main fire path (`generator.py:1424`) never writes it.

**Design 2 as briefed cannot be built.** It needs an anchor that the fire path does not currently produce.

## C. The design, revised to survive B

### C.1 Anchor — use what already exists

**Anchor on the ~9 beliefs `_retrieve_context_beliefs` already returns.** They are real belief ids, already in hand at the call site, and require no new machinery or revival of `anchor_belief_id`. This changes demand to `529 × 9 × F`.

### C.2 Refractory window — the arithmetic

With 9 anchors and fan-out 1 per anchor: **demand = 4,762 traversals/day** against the full 135,801-edge pool → **28.5-day full cycle**.

**Proposed window: 7 days.**

- At 4,762/day, a 7-day window holds at most **33,334 edges in refractory = 24.5% of the pool**.
- **75.5% of edges remain eligible at all times** — no exhaustion, so traversal never silently returns nothing.
- No edge can be traversed more than **52 times/year**.
- The window is **4× shorter than the natural cycle (28.5 d)**, so it is not the binding constraint — pool size is. That is the correct relationship, and it is the one the semantic-subset design fails.

**Edge selection: `weight ≥ 0.8` (75,177 edges), giving a 15.8-day cycle at fan-out 1.** Chosen over the semantic filter because B.3 shows the semantic subset is both too small (B.2) and disjoint from any weight filter. **This is a real compromise and should be stated as one:** it buys pool size at the cost of semantic quality, and the resulting neighbours will be token-overlap-similar rather than reasoned relations.

### C.3 Shape, in code terms — pseudocode only, nothing written

```
after _retrieve_context_beliefs returns `rows`:
  anchors  = [r.id for r in rows]                      # ~9
  eligible = SELECT target_id, id FROM belief_edges
             WHERE source_id IN anchors
               AND weight >= 0.8
               AND (last_traversed_at IS NULL
                    OR last_traversed_at < now - 7d)
             ORDER BY RANDOM()                          # NOT by weight - see A.2
             LIMIT fan_out                              # hard cap, guards max out-degree 630
  if eligible is empty:  return rows unchanged          # C.4 fallback
  mark   UPDATE belief_edges SET last_traversed_at = now WHERE id IN (...)
  return rows + neighbours   AS A DEDICATED SLOT        # NOT merged - see A.3
```

Three precedent-driven choices are load-bearing:

- **`ORDER BY RANDOM()`, never `ORDER BY weight`** — A.2. No stored score ranks the queue, so no ghost can form.
- **Dedicated slot, never merged into the oversample** — A.3.
- **`last_traversed_at` written in the same statement scope as the read** — the damper reads and writes `belief_edges`, satisfying R44's rule, and R45's added clause is satisfied by C.2's 24.5%-occupancy arithmetic.

### C.4 Fallback for the 31.5% orphans

**Return `rows` unchanged.** The traversal is purely additive: if an anchor has no eligible edge, the fire proceeds with exactly today's retrieval. This is the `_is_on_subject` pattern (R29) — failure yields current behaviour, never a permissive branch. With 31.5% of beliefs orphaned and 9 anchors per fire, **P(all 9 orphaned) ≈ 0.315⁹ ≈ 3×10⁻⁵** — the fallback is a safety net, not a common path.

### C.5 Offline prediction and falsifier

**Prediction — REASONED.** Retrieval output composition gains 1–2 neighbour beliefs per fire in a dedicated slot, displacing nothing (additive). Expect the **source mix of retrieved beliefs to shift toward older material**, since edges connect across time while the existing query is recency-ordered. Quantitatively: neighbours drawn at `weight ≥ 0.8` are token-overlap-similar to their anchors, so **expect an increase in maxDF\* of the crystallized corpus** — this design's most likely harm is convergence, not grooving.

**Falsifiers, pre-registered:**

1. **Re-concentration:** distinct edges traversed per 7-day window < 50% of demand (i.e. < ~16,700 of 33,334) → the traversal is revisiting a subset and the window is not holding.
2. **Exhaustion:** eligible-set empty on > 5% of fires → pool too small or window too long.
3. **Convergence harm:** maxDF\* rises > 5 points against the adjacent pre-window (R36 §3.1 rule — adjacent, never pooled) → the neighbours are amplifying topic concentration.
4. **Groove:** any single belief appearing as a traversed neighbour in > 2% of fires.
