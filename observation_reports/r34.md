**Two readings, not interchangeable:**

| reading | definition | why both |
|---|---|---|
| `on_subject` | binary, ≥1 shared content token | exactly the predicate `crystallizer._is_on_subject` **already** applies on the fire path to choose the 600 vs 300 char ceiling (R29). Reported so the new metric is commensurable with the gate that already exists. |
| `recall` | continuous, \|focal tokens in fire\| / \|focal tokens\| | strictly more informative — a fire can share one incidental token and still be about something else entirely. The 20% threshold is the R33 reporting convention, **not** a gate. |

Tokenizer is `crystallizer._fidelity_tokens`, the house standard, so readings are comparable with maxDF*. This shifts R33's figures slightly, which used an ad-hoc 4-char tokenizer: P(zero overlap) reads **0.471** here vs **0.469** there.

Fires with **no** `focal_item` (DRIFT, substrate voice) bind no subject by design and are **excluded from the denominator**. Including them would make this a measure of mode mix, not of fidelity.

### 3.1 The series is NOT stationary — read this before comparing anything

This is the most important finding of the round and it is a caveat on my own metric.

| window | n | on-subject | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| T−4d..T−3d | 291 | 0.454 | [0.397, 0.511] |
| T−3d..T−2d | 325 | 0.523 | [0.469, 0.577] |
| T−2d..T−1d | 306 | 0.588 | [0.532, 0.642] |
| T−1d..T (adjacent) | 312 | 0.567 | [0.512, 0.621] |
| **T..T+1d (post-R32)** | **155** | **0.639** | **[0.561, 0.710]** |

On-subject was **already climbing ~4 points/day before any round-34 work**, from a cause not yet identified. The post-R32 reading continues that trend, and its CI overlaps the adjacent pre-restart day heavily.

**R32 cannot be credited with the fidelity improvement.** I initially read the post-R32 window against the pooled 7-day baseline, saw non-overlapping intervals, and nearly reported R32 as having improved fidelity. That comparison is confounded: the pool averages in the low early days. Against the *adjacent* day there is no significant difference.

A single-point comparison against the pooled figure **will manufacture a false positive**. Judge any intervention against the adjacent pre-window or a fitted trend. This caveat is written into the module's `BASELINE` block, not just here.

**Unexplained, and worth a future round:** what is driving the ~4 points/day climb? It predates R32 and no round has claimed it.
