# **The reason is structural and is the finding of this round: at 81 docs/day, every document you add buys sample size AND costs elapsed time.** W 100→500 gains **+10.4** points of sampling stability and loses **−7.5** to drift. They nearly cancel. **The corpus drifts about as fast as it accumulates.**

## C. VERDICT 2 — RED — OBSERVED

### An identification problem I hit and fixed

My first projection regressed retention on `ln(W)` and `lag` jointly using
adjacent-window pairs. **In that design `lag ≡ W/81` by construction, so the two
regressors are deterministically related and their coefficients are not separately
identified.** It returned R(500) = 21.0. I discarded it and re-identified:

- **drift slope** from pairs at **fixed W**, varying lag → clean, n=1,653 pairs at W=100
- **n-effect** from pairs at **matched lag** (1.5–2.5 d band), varying W → clean

| | |
|---|---|
| n-effect (matched lag) | R = −7.20 + **6.43·ln(W)** → **+10.4** points for W×5 |
| drift slope (fixed W) | **−1.80** terms/day (−1.40 at W=60, −1.68 at W=100, −2.31 at W=160) |

**The drift slope does not fall as W grows.** A bigger fit does not average drift away,
because the two fits being compared remain separated in time.

### The projection

| | |
|---|---|
| anchor: W=100, lag=2.0 d | R = 22.12 |
| n-gain, W 100→500 | **+10.4** |

| **projected R(500)** | **25.0 / 40** |

Both identifications — the badly conditioned one (21.0) and the clean one (25.0) —

## D. What does not work — OBSERVED, including one of my own hypotheses

| candidate | result | |
|---|---|---|
| **more documents** | projected 25.0/40 | fails |
| **decaying-weight fit** (the fix my pre-registration named for TREND) | optimum half-life **2.5 d**, ceiling **~26.1/40** | **fails** |
| **smaller K** | fractional retention *worse* at small K — 57.8% (K=10), 53.9% (K=20), 65.6% (K=40) | fails |
| **DF threshold instead of rank cutoff** | best Jaccard 0.483 (DF≥8%) vs rank-40's **0.490** | **fails** |

**The DF-threshold test was my own hypothesis and it was wrong.** I reasoned that a
rank cutoff forces exactly 40 terms, so a term drifting across the boundary displaces
another — "musical chairs" — and that a threshold would avoid it. Measured: no scheme
beat the rank cutoff. **The instability is genuine vocabulary turnover distributed
through the list, not a cutoff artefact.**

**And the pre-registration's own remedy for TREND is insufficient.** The discriminator
worked; the fix it implies does not reach the bar. Worth stating plainly, because the
pre-registration presented decaying-weight as *the* answer for trend.

## E. What does work — persistence filtering — OBSERVED

Per-rank survival into the other half's top-40 shows the structure:

| ranks | survival |
|---|---|
| 1–8 | **100%** |
| 9–16 | 75% |
| 17–24 | 44% |
| 25–32 | 50% |
| 33–40 | 38% |

**There is a stable core and a churning tail.** So select for persistence rather than
for rank: keep only terms in the top-40 of **all k disjoint sub-windows** of the
fitting corpus.

| k | list size | survives in held-out half | **survival rate** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (today's method) | 40 | 27 | 67.5% |
| **2** | 23 | 19 | **82.6%** |
| **3** | 18 | 15 | **83.3%** |
| **4** | 13 | 12 | **92.3%** |
| 5 | 9 | 8 | 88.9% |

**R31's bar in rate terms is 82.5%. k≥2 clears it.**

The k=3 core (18 terms):

```
aligns, being, belief, discusses, emerging, engagement, highlights, item, like, quiet,
recent, such, tech, through, towards, understanding, within, without
```

It contains exactly the staleness misses R59 identified (`aligns`, `discusses`, `item`,
`engagement`) alongside genuine long-standing register (`quiet`, `like`, `recent`,
`through`, `within`, `without`, `tech`, `emerging`).

**It is not a drop-in.** A smaller exclusion set means fewer terms removed from
contention, so readings rise. **List, quantiles and threshold must be derived in one
operation** — which was already R61's recommendation for a different reason.

## F. Recommendation

1. **Do not treat Aug 21 as a refit date. Throughput cannot reach the bar** —
   3.3–5× would be needed, and volume is regulated at ~82/day (R58).
2. **The next round is a design round, not a wait.** Its subject: persistence-filtered

   list. It needs its own pre-registration, including a decision on list size — k=4
   gives 92.3% survival on 13 terms, and a 13-term register may under-exclude.

   documents were 9.6 content tokens and the register was a small set of mood words.

   **A stability bar transplanted across that regime change is the same category error
   this series has now found three times** (§5's quantiles in R61, the "1–7 points"
   headroom in R60, the 1,000-doc floor in R62).
4. **Interim posture unchanged:** maxDF\* stays un-consulted, which costs nothing —
   R62 verified nothing reads it.
