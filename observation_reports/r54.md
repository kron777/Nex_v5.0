## A. The re-weighting question — DROPPED, premise falsified — OBSERVED

R53 flagged that 61.7% of chat queries surface no tier-6 belief and attributed it to confidence multiplying into the score, keystones at 0.95–1.00 outranking conclusions at 0.60–0.92. **I tested that directly on the same 120 real queries.**

**A.1 — is it chat-path-only?** Yes. `BeliefRetriever.retrieve` is the same function R53 changed, single caller `stage_capability/tools.py:169`, no reference in `generator.py`/`crystallizer.py`/`run.py`. Safe in principle.

**A.2 — weighting, or over-confident keystones? NEITHER.** Three weightings, top-5, 120 real queries:

| scoring | tier-6/query | queries with ≥1 tier-6 |
|---|---|---|
| **shipped** `rel × confidence` | **0.97** | 36.7% |
| damped `rel × √confidence` | **0.97** | 35.8% |
| **relevance only** (confidence removed) | **0.97** | 35.8% |

**Deleting confidence entirely changes nothing, and slightly *reduces* the tier-6 hit rate.** Confidence spans 0.60–1.00 — a 1.67× range — against an overlap ratio spanning 0–1. **It was never the discriminating term.** R53's stated mechanism is wrong.

I tested the obvious alternative — that short generic keystones win on `overlap / |query|` — and **that fails too**: top-ranked beliefs average **27.3** content tokens against a pool mean of 19.3, and tier-1 keystones (14.4) are indistinguishable from tier-6 conclusions (15.0). **Longer, not shorter.**

**A.3 — what is actually going on, and why there is nothing to fix.** Splitting the 120 queries by whether they are about NEX itself:

| query type | n | tier-6 in top-5 | queries with ≥1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **self-referential** (*"why are you restless nex?"*) | 81 | 0.49 | **23.5%** |
| **topical** | 39 | **1.97** | **64.1%** |

**Two-thirds of Jon's recent chat messages are about NEX itself, and for those, identity keystones are the correct answer.** On topical queries — where NEX's own conclusions *are* the right answer — the retriever already surfaces them 64% of the time.

**So R53's 61.7% was an artefact of query mix. There is no defect here, and a re-weighting would degrade the case the current behaviour gets right.** No change designed, none shipped.

**One further reason it would have been wrong to ship even if it had value:** R53's pool change to this same function is **still staged-inactive and has never run in production**. A second change to the same code path would put two variables on one activation — the R31 confound, self-inflicted.

## B. The harness — built, validated, run

### B.1 Design

`scripts/replay_retrieval.py`. **Paired A/B over real fires.** For each sampled fire it rebuilds the prompt twice, identical except the belief-context block:

- **`recency`** — the 9 most recent own-content beliefs as of that fire, reproducing `_retrieve_context_beliefs`' ordering. What the fountain actually did.
- **`relevance`** — the 9 highest token-overlap with that fire's `focal_item` over a 30-day window. What R52 §D proposes.

Both generated against **the same model the fountain uses** (`qwen2.5:3b` at `NEX5_VOICE_URL`), then scored with **live audit predicates, imported not reimplemented**:

| metric | source | why |
|---|---|---|
| `on_subject` | `crystallizer._is_on_subject` | the audit's fidelity standard since R34 |
| `engagement_fail` | `crystallizer._has_engagement` | the crystallizer's actual admission gate |
| `novelty_F2` | `score_v2.feat_anti_template` | the scorer's own novelty feature |
| `chars` / `too_long` | 600-char R29 ceiling | the *first* crystallization gate |

Paired per-fire deltas with a 5,000-rep bootstrap CI.

**What it is not, stated in the docstring:** not a full-prompt replay. Production carries ~15 blocks; this builds mode template + item + belief block. **It is a differential instrument — read the delta, not the level.**

### B.2 Validation — three checks, all passed — OBSERVED

R47's harness was validated against a frozen baseline before use; same discipline here.

**1. The imported predicates reproduce a known result.** Applied to the live fires in the window:

| mode | n | on-subject | engagement-fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| EXPLAIN | 524 | **0.779** [0.741, 0.812] | **0.469** [0.427, 0.512] |
| ARGUE | 533 | 0.456 | 0.075 |

**R47 measured EXPLAIN post-C2 at on-subject 0.785 and engagement-fail 0.455.** Both inside these CIs.

**2. Live model reachable** — `qwen2.5:3b`, 0.48 s round-trip.

**3. The arms differ as intended, before spending any generation:** recency **0.50 of 9** relevant with **63.3%** zero-relevant fires; relevance **8.54 of 9** with **0.0%**. R52 §A.2 measured 0.62/59.2% and 8.87/0% — agreement within sampling.

**A bug caught by validation 3, and worth recording.** My first pass read 1.98 and 36.7% — ~3× R52's figure — because I had inlined a local tokenizer instead of importing `_fidelity_tokens`. That is precisely the drift `GENIUS_FIDELITY_BASELINE.md` §1's one-definition rule exists to prevent, **in the very file whose docstring cites that rule.** Fixed, and a comment now blocks re-inlining. **Validation earned its place before the harness produced a single number.**

### B.3 First result — **a NULL** — OBSERVED

40 fires × 2 arms = 80 generations, 1,501 s.

| metric | recency | relevance | paired delta | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `on_subject` | 0.925 [0.801, 0.974] | 0.975 [0.871, 0.996] | **+0.0500** | [−0.0500, +0.1500] |
| `engagement_fail` | 0.325 [0.201, 0.480] | 0.275 [0.161, 0.428] | **−0.0500** | [−0.2250, +0.1250] |
| `novelty_F2` | 0.999 | 0.999 | −0.0001 | [−0.0005, +0.0004] |
| `chars` | 439.0 | 465.6 | +26.6 | [−26.45, +81.45] |
| **`too_long`** | 0.125 [0.055, 0.261] | **0.200** [0.105, 0.348] | **+0.0750** | [−0.0500, +0.2000] |

**Every CI includes zero. At n=40 there is no detectable effect of relevance retrieval on the generated thought.**

Direction is mildly favourable on the two metrics that matter (+5 pp on-subject, −5 pp engagement-failure) and **unfavourable on length**: outputs run 27 chars longer and `too_long` rises 0.125 → 0.200. **`too_long` is the *first* crystallization gate, so if that holds it would reduce crystallization — the opposite of the intent.** It is the specific risk R54 must rule out, and nothing before this harness could have seen it.

**Power — what a decisive run costs:**

| metric | sd(diff) | n to resolve | generations | wall-clock @ ~19 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `too_long` | 0.417 | 475 | 950 | ~5 h |
| **`on_subject`** | 0.316 | **615** | **1,230** | **~6.5 h** |
| `chars` | 178.2 | 689 | 1,378 | ~7 h |
| `engagement_fail` | 0.552 | 1,876 | 3,752 | ~20 h |

**R54's real precondition is now a number: ~615 fires / 1,230 generations, roughly a 6-hour run**, and engagement-failure is out of reach in a single session — that endpoint should be dropped or measured live post-ship rather than offline.

**This is the harness doing its job.** R52 §D predicted relevance retrieval would improve thoughts; the first evidence that could speak to it says *not detectably, and watch the length gate*. **R54 is no longer a foregone conclusion, which is exactly why this was worth building before the refit landed rather than after.**

## C. The drives cleanup — design only, and do NOT stage — OBSERVED + REASONED

**C.1 — is it fire-path? YES.** `generator.py:2254` calls `self._competing_drives.format_for_prompt()` and appends it to `prompt_parts`. Drive weights enter the prompt. **It needs a restart and therefore cannot ship before the refit.**

**C.2 — offline prediction, from the live inputs right now:**

```
_contradiction_pressure = min(1, 0/5.0)  = 0.0000     (0 opposes edges in 24 h)
_open_problems_pressure = min(1, 0/20.0) = 0.0000     (0 of 434 problems open)

integ = contradiction*0.4 + affect_variance*0.4 + comp_arcs*0.2
cur   = problems*0.35    + content_complexity*0.35 + probes*0.3
```

**Two removal forms, and they are not equivalent — this is the part worth getting right:**

- **(a) delete the term, do not renormalise the remaining coefficients** → `integ` and `cur` both unchanged, because **both terms currently contribute exactly 0.0000**. After the cross-drive normalise-to-sum-1.0, **the change is an exact no-op at present values.**
- **(b) delete and renormalise within the drive** (0.4/0.6, 0.35/0.65) → `integ` ×1.667, `cur` ×1.54. **A large change to two of five drives, dressed as cleanup.**

**Form (a) is the only honest one, and it buys nothing measurable — it is code hygiene, not behaviour.**

**C.3 — I am departing from the brief here, and saying so.** The brief suggested staging it to ride the restart that activates R53's pool fix. **I recommend not staging it.** That restart is also the one R54 will need. R53's pool change is chat-path and cannot confound a fountain endpoint — but a drives change *writes into the prompt*, so it would sit inside R54's window and contaminate the one measurement this entire sequence has been built to make. **Rounds 31 and 38 both had to declare a contaminating restart after the fact; this one is avoidable in advance, for a change measured at exactly zero.**

**Recommendation: do it as its own trivial round after R54 reads out, in form (a), or fold it into a restart that has no live endpoint.** Nothing was written.
