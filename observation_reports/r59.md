The pre-registered metric counts **distinct tokens**. Weighting instead by **actual
blocking events** (`crystallization_rejects.matched_pattern`, 127 blocks since R57)
gives a different picture, and honesty requires reporting it:

**≈110 of 127 blocks (86.6%) trace to scaffolding fragments, not topical ones.**

The two views are both true and not in conflict. Groove writes many one-off cooldowns
on topical fragments that never re-fire (dominating the *token* count), while a handful
of scaffolding phrases sit in nearly every EXPLAIN/ARGUE output and block *repeatedly*
(dominating the *event* count). And the tokens in those top fragments — `engagement`,
`item`, `discusses`, `recent`, `aligns` — are exactly maxDF\*'s current false-breach
drivers.

**What this does and does not license.** It does not overturn §B. The set-level claim
R58 proposed — that these are one problem — is **false**, and a metric selected after
seeing the data cannot be used to rescue it. What it does support is a *narrower*
claim the pre-registration never tested: **a handful of specific scaffolding phrases
are doing disproportionate damage in both systems at once.** That is a different
hypothesis, and it needs its own pre-registration before anyone acts on it.
